PeNNy-937 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

PeNNy-937 is an animation app for Android, published by Dahl404 (“the app”, “we”). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.
  • It has no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • It has no user accounts and no sign-in.
  • It holds no INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of sending data off your device.

What is stored on your device

  • Projects you create: drawings, frames, layers and settings, saved as vector documents inside the app's private storage.
  • Reference media you import (images and video frames), copied into the app's private storage so the project can be reopened later.
  • Preferences such as the last opened project and tool settings.

All of this stays in the app's private directory. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Nothing here is sent off the device. If you have Android's system backup enabled, the system may back up the app's data to your own Google account under your own settings; reference media is excluded from that backup by the app. That backup is controlled by your device, not by us, and we never receive a copy of it.

Files you choose to import

Images, videos and project zips are opened through Android's system file picker. The app only receives the file you select, and it copies what it needs into its private storage. It does not browse or read the rest of your device.

Files you choose to export or share

When you export a movie, GIF, PNG or project zip, the file is written to the location you pick or handed to the app you pick from Android's share sheet. What happens to it after that is governed by the app or service you chose, not by PeNNy-937.

Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions at runtime and declares none in its manifest. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar or accounts.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no data at all, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new “last updated” date. If a future version of the app ever handles data differently, that change will be described here before it ships.
